Nvidia's board voted unanimously to reinstate **Jensen Huang** as president and CEO on 23 November, effective immediately, eleven days after he resigned. The board said it had "received input from senior levels of the United States government" and concluded that his return poses no national security risk. Nvidia shares closed up **31%**, recovering most of the roughly 33% the stock lost after Huang admitted an extramarital affair on 8 November.

The independent committee and the law firm **Wachtell, Lipton, Rosen & Katz** continue their investigation, and Huang has said he will cooperate. He is due for a transcribed interview with three House committees on 1 December, Nvidia general counsel **Tim Teter** on 2 December, and a public Senate Banking hearing on 3 December where Huang is the only witness. Senator **Elizabeth Warren** called the reinstatement reckless and said the company still owes Congress answers about export-control compliance.
