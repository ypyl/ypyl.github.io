Anthropic has released **Claude Sonnet 5.5**, positioned for coding, agentic tasks, and everyday work. The model is more than 30% faster than Sonnet 5 and up to 30% cheaper per task through lower token consumption. The gains are largest in programming: 70.6% on Terminal-Bench 4.0, up from 10.3% for Sonnet 5, and 55.5% on CursorBench 4.0, roughly two points behind Opus 5.5. On GDPval-AA it nearly matches Opus 5.5.

Anthropic says the model works through codebases faster, makes fewer tool calls, and takes fewer steps per task. It also handles long-horizon tasks, images, documents, spreadsheets, and presentations better than its predecessor. API pricing is $2 per million input tokens, $10 per million output tokens, and $0.20 per million cache reads. Sonnet 5.5 is available in Claude, the API, AWS, Google Cloud, and Azure under the model ID `claude-sonnet-5-5`.
