MiniMax has announced **M3.1 Flash**, a model it says runs three times faster than **M3** at a third of the cost, with open weights due on **October 23**. The company confirmed that **Space Bunny**, the model that recently topped global leaderboards after training on more than 200 trillion tokens, was an early version of M3.1 Flash. MiniMax reports scores of **62% on Automation-Bench**, **30% on ALE**, **74% on DeepSWE**, and **91% on BabyVision**. API pricing is **$0.15 per million input tokens**, **$0.60 per million output tokens**, and **$0.015 per million cached input tokens**, available through the [MiniMax API](https://platform.minimax.io/), [M Plan](https://minimax.io/m-plan), or the [MiniMax Code](https://agent.minimax.io/download) desktop app.
