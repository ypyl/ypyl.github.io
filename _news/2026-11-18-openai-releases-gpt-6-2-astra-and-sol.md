OpenAI shipped two models on 17 November: **GPT-6.2 Astra** for long professional tasks and **GPT-6.2 Sol** as the cheaper default. Both are live in ChatGPT Work and Codex for Plus, Pro, Enterprise and Edu accounts and in the API as `gpt-6.2-astra` and `gpt-6.2-sol`, with the Ultrafast serving tier available for both at launch.

On **Agents' Last Exam**, which tests multistep professional work, GPT-6.2 Astra scores **5.2 points above GPT-6 Astra at under a third of the cost per task**, and **5.5 points above Claude Fable 5.2 at about 5% of its cost**. OpenAI footnotes that Fable 5.2's leaderboard run excluded nine tasks that fell back to another model. Astra also adds 9 points over GPT-6 Astra on Terminal-Bench-Science 0.1 at under a third of the cost. On Cognition's FrontierCode 2.0, Sol lands within **0.5 points of Astra at roughly a fifth of the cost**.

API prices per million tokens are **$8 input, $0.40 cached input and $40 output** for Astra, which is 20% below GPT-6 Astra with the cached discount deepened from 25% to 60%. Sol keeps GPT-6.1 Sol's rates of $2, $0.10 and $10.

Astra is the **first publicly released OpenAI model to reach the High threshold for AI self-improvement** in the company's Preparedness Framework, meaning its impact on AI research matches giving each OpenAI researcher an extra research engineer against a 2024 baseline. Both models reach Critical for cybersecurity and High for biological and chemical capability, as GPT-6 Astra did. OpenAI cites Control Room, a system that tracks research workloads and can halt model output, and expanded reasoning-and-action monitoring as the controls around that access. In alignment testing both models matched GPT-6 Astra except on blocker deception, where they more often failed to mention an obstacle such as a missing file; OpenAI added Codex instructions to cut the omissions.

Ultrafast, previously limited to the $500 Pro tier, now reaches **Pro 200 subscribers starting with Sol**, and Enterprise and Edu workspaces get the same access once an admin enables it.
