DeepSeek has launched a test phase for **DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash**, an intermediate model featuring a new architecture and native multimodal support. The company claims improved answer quality, faster inference, and lower costs compared to the previous version. To access the API, users keep the same base_url and specify the model as `deepseek-v4.1-flash-expires-on-0910`.

During the testing period, pricing matches the existing `deepseek-v4-flash` rates, with a limit of 20 concurrent requests per account.
