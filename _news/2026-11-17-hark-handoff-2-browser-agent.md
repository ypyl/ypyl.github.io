Hark, the consumer AI lab founded by Figure AI's Brett Adcock, shipped the second generation of its computer-use agent on 12 November. On the company's internal browsing benchmark of **478 tasks across 238 websites**, Handoff² completes **82%, up from 73%** for the first version, and finishes each task **46% faster**.

With backend changes folded in, the agent averages twice the speed of v1, reaches 3.8x faster on flight booking and more than 5x on long research tasks. Hark says accuracy lands within 5 points of Claude Opus 5.5 at max effort running in the same harness. Every figure here is the company's own measurement.

Since the October launch, Handoff has logged 5.6 million browsing sessions and handled more than $20 million in purchases. The new version is rolling out to every Hark Pro plan.
