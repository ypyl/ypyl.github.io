Anthropic's public **S-1 filing** puts numbers on its enterprise business for the first time. As of September 30, 2026, more than **11,900 customers** spent at least $100,000 on Claude over the trailing 12 months, more than **1,900** spent at least $1 million, and about **250** spent at least $10 million each. That $100,000+ group includes **all 10 Fortune 10 companies**, more than half of the **Fortune 500**, and over 30% of the **Global 2000**.

The large-account share of revenue is shrinking as the base widens: customers spending $100,000+ produced **76% of 2025 revenue**, roughly **$3.5 billion**, down from **90% in 2023**. Their headcount rose from about **20 at the end of 2023** to **1,500 at the end of 2025**, so the lower share came from faster growth elsewhere, not from losing large customers.
