Nvidia has agreed to acquire **Hugging Face** for **$12.9 billion**, according to The Information — with the sides finalizing the deal and no official comments from either company yet. Talks accelerated after Hugging Face received a takeover offer from another unnamed player and hired a bank to assess demand. For Nvidia, the deal is a move beyond hardware: direct control over the platform where most open models are developed and distributed. The acquisition will almost certainly draw antitrust scrutiny.
