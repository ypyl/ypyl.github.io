The Trump administration plans to designate **more than two dozen US AI companies as "national champions"**, the Wall Street Journal reported on 16 November, with Nvidia, Amazon, Microsoft, OpenAI and Anthropic among those set to be named, according to people familiar with the matter. No official announcement has been made, and neither the final list nor what the designation entitles a company to has been published.

The Journal's report is the only sourcing so far: Reuters relayed it the same day, and the administration has not confirmed the list, the criteria or what a designated company gets.

That gap matters for Nvidia in particular, whose board is weighing whether chief executive Jensen Huang stays while shareholders sue over his departure. Treat the list as unconfirmed until the announcement.
