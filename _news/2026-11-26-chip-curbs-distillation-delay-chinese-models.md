Reuters [reports](https://www.reuters.com/world/china/us-chip-curbs-distillation-crackdown-threaten-chinese-ai-model-development-2026-11-26/) that Z.AI has fallen behind schedule on **GLM-5.5**, its next flagship, which was planned for release this month and is designed at roughly **3 trillion parameters**. One of the two people cited says the delay is partly the fault of increasingly effective safeguards against distillation. Z.AI shipped GLM-5.4 on Monday, built on an existing 750 billion parameter model, and says the new design matches models two to three times its size.

Moonshot AI, which rents overseas compute and prioritises foreign providers, expects its usual **six-month gap between major releases to stretch to 12 months or longer** if that access is cut off entirely, with **Kimi K4 possibly slipping to mid-2027**. With outputs from Anthropic and OpenAI harder to obtain, the company has started distilling from Google and Meta models. Anthropic said in September it had seen nearly **200 million exchanges** linked to suspected distillation campaigns and attributed them to Chinese developers including Alibaba, Moonshot and Z.AI.

The constraint has two independent halves: access to frontier accelerators, and the loss of a cheap way to train. On the second, the person familiar with Moonshot's operations said computing capacity available inside China could not make up the shortfall.
