title: "Nvidia Carries Only $900M of Side A Cover as Shareholders Sue Over Huang's Exit"
date: 2026-11-15

---

Nvidia closed down 4.3% on Thursday, wiping out almost **$200 billion** of market value, after founder and CEO **Jensen Huang** resigned over two extramarital affairs, one with a woman US authorities believe acted as a Chinese spy. A shareholder filed a proposed securities class action against Nvidia and Huang on November 9, and the Financial Times reports the company bought only **Side A** directors and officers cover: a $900 million policy, placed through its broker with Beazley, AIG and Chubb among the insurers, that protects the personal assets of directors and executives but not the company's own balance sheet.

Side B, which reimburses the company when it indemnifies its executives, and Side C, which answers claims against the company itself, are both missing from the policy. A 2018 WTW survey found about 91% of public companies bought dedicated D&O cover and more than 80% took Side B and Side C as well as Side A, which is why the lawyers and brokers FT spoke to called the arrangement rare. Nvidia now funds its own defence and any settlement from operating cash.
