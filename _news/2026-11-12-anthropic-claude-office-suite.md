Anthropic has introduced a set of office tools for Claude: **Claude Meetings**, which joins work calls and writes up notes, **Claude Sheets** for editing spreadsheets, and **Claude Whiteboards** for sketching ideas and turning presentations into explainer videos. The three are in beta for paid and enterprise subscribers, while the analytics module **Claude Data** has shipped in general release.

Alongside them, Anthropic has built interactive connectors on top of **MCP** for HubSpot, Adobe, Atlassian and other services, so Claude reads and acts on the same data the tools cover. The pitch is continuity across a workday rather than a separate product for each task.
