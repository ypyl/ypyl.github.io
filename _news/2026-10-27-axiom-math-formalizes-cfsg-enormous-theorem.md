Axiom Math says it has produced the first computer-checkable proof of the **classification of finite simple groups (CFSG)**, the result known as the "enormous theorem", whose original proof runs to roughly **15,000 pages** across more than **500 papers** built by about **100 mathematicians** over decades. In **24 days**, dozens of AI agents wrote around **43.1 million lines of Lean** code to formalize that body of work, with mathematicians involved in the original classification taking part.

The process did not just transcribe. Axiom reports **34 substantive discrepancies** with published mathematics, counterexamples to some claims, alternative proofs where the originals broke, a new group-theory result needed for its chosen route, and cases where agents corrected faulty reasoning and supplied the missing argument. The code and materials are open for independent review.
