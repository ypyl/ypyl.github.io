Leading AI companies are regularly briefing employees on how not to become a source of leaks, according to a **Wall Street Journal** report. **OpenAI** warns its teams about bribery, eavesdropping in cafes, cleaning crews that may have been compromised, and honey traps built around seduction; **Anthropic** and **Google** staff have received similar briefings.

The OpenAI rules are concrete: outside the office, hide anything carrying the company logo, and treat kitchen and cleaning staff as possible leak channels. Reminders arrive on a schedule, including through Slack.

The paranoia has a price tag behind it. A handful of labs hold a concentration of value, several have large **IPOs** approaching, and the WSJ puts the valuations discussed at roughly **$3.5 trillion**. Model weights, training data and architectural decisions are now valuable enough to attract the classic methods of espionage.
