date: 2026-08-30

Quantum computing company **QuEra** connected a **Claude** AI agent directly to real laboratory equipment via Anthropic's **Model Hardware Standard**, letting it modify control code, run experiments, and adjust its strategy in real time. The agent recovered the laser frequency needed to keep atom-based qubits stable with a **99.3% success rate** (695 of 700 tests) across seven disturbance types, fixing simple drift in 0.9–5.4 seconds and complex mode-hop events in 10–14 seconds—including all 43 natural mode-hops without human help.

Claude then optimized 12 coupled laser control parameters over ~12,500 iterations, cutting residual frequency noise roughly **5×** and surviving a **19-hour autonomous test** with zero lock losses (down from ~1.6/hour before optimization). In one night it also tuned a second laser band that normally takes specialists weeks of manual work.
