DeepSeek users started hitting new limits in the app and on the website on Sunday, 15 November, TechNode reported. A notice says long conversations inactive for more than **seven days** can no longer be resumed because of insufficient computing capacity. Users also report prompts capped at about **40,000 Chinese characters** and a usage-limit message once a daily quota runs out.

DeepSeek has not published the limits or a date for lifting them. Its status page lists eight incidents since October affecting chat or the V4 Pro and V4.1 Flash APIs. The caps affect the free consumer chat; API access runs under separate rules.

Anyone keeping long working conversations in DeepSeek should export them before the next cap lands.
