Sweden's **Government Offices (Regeringskansliet)** has restricted staff access to public AI services that lack a separate contract for handling sensitive data, and its director of digitalization framed the move as a risk to the protection of government information. Per Swedish Radio's **Ekot**, the **Swedish Tax Agency** and the **National Board of Institutional Care (SiS)** have already enforced blocks, and the **Enforcement Authority** is preparing the same rules.

Audits prompted the decision: staff were routinely uploading work material to about ten public platforms.

Rather than give up the automation gains, the Government Offices is moving staff onto its own infrastructure, where remaining **Claude** and **ChatGPT** use will run only through a secure internal platform alongside the corporate **Microsoft Copilot**.
