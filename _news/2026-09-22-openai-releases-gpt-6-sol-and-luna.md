OpenAI has expanded its GPT-6 family with two new models: **GPT-6 Sol**, a powerful workhorse for complex tasks, and **GPT-6 Luna**, a faster, cheaper variant built for scale and automation. Both are built on the GPT-6 Astra foundation but optimized for inference efficiency and lower cost. API pricing for Sol starts at $2 per million input tokens and $10 per million output tokens — 50% cheaper than GPT-5.6's promo rates — and the models show notably better quality-to-cost ratios on AutomationBench. The lineup now covers the full range: Astra for maximum capability, Sol for serious daily work, and Luna for speed, scale, and low cost.
