An unannounced image generator called **Mona-lisa-1** has appeared on the Arena platform, and the community has confirmed its OpenAI origin: the company's official verification service detected hidden **SynthID** watermarks in the generated images — a signal so rarely false-positive that observers are confident OpenAI is testing the successor to **GPT Image 2**. Early testers report the model follows complex instructions noticeably more accurately and renders in-image text far better than the current model, clearly outperforming it overall, though some complain about noise and texture artifacts — issues that plagued the previous generation. OpenAI has made no official comment.
