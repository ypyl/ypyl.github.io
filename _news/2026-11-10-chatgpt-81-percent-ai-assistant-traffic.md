date: 2026-11-10

**ChatGPT** carried **81.4 percent** of global AI assistant traffic, according to an analysis by **Graphite** reported by Business Insider, ahead of Claude at **6.8 percent**, Google Gemini at **5 percent**, DeepSeek at **4 percent**, Grok at **1.9 percent** and Perplexity at **0.9 percent**. ChatGPT drew a record **61.35 billion visits** in September, up almost **20 percent** after seven months without growth, while visits to AI services overall rose **37 percent** year over year to more than **76 billion** a month.

Visitors from the United States made up only **8.8 percent** of that traffic. Referrals from AI platforms to outside websites grew **92 percent**, and ChatGPT supplied **60 percent** of them.
