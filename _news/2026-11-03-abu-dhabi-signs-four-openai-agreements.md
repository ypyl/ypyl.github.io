title: "Abu Dhabi Signs Four OpenAI Agreements to Move Government Work Onto AI"
date: 2026-11-03

---

Sheikh **Khaled bin Mohamed bin Zayed** witnessed the signing of four agreements with **OpenAI** in the presence of **Sam Altman**, with the emirate already rewriting its government regulations into formats models can work from. The pilot covers **food import licences and certificates**: an algorithm checks each application against the rules and returns a preliminary finding that cites the specific law behind it, while the final decision stays with the official. OpenAI says all inference runs inside **UAE data centres on a local model service**, keeping the processing in-country.

The remaining three agreements spread the models across the emirate's institutions:

- **Mubadala**, the sovereign investment fund, will use them to assess risk and search for prospective assets.
- **G42** holds a five-year contract to deploy OpenAI models in education, business and the public sector.
- **ADNOC**, the state oil company, receives access to the **OpenAI Frontier** platform.

UAE investors rank among the largest backers of funding rounds at both OpenAI and Anthropic, which gives the agreements a commercial dimension beyond procurement.
