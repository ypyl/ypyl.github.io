OpenAI has launched **Work, in Progress**, a curated collection of real-world scenarios, experiments, and prompts for ChatGPT and Codex submitted by developers and everyday users. The library covers a wide range of applications including building websites and APIs, working with multiple AI agents, document analysis and research, code review and debugging, video editing through Codex, and everyday task automation. Materials can be filtered by product, task type, author, and language, making it a practical idea base for anyone looking to integrate ChatGPT and Codex into their workflow.
