Satya Nadella posted photos of the **first serial Nvidia Vera Rubin racks** arriving at Microsoft data centers, and Nvidia confirmed the platform has entered full-scale production. Vera Rubin is the rack-scale AI platform that succeeds Blackwell: each **NVL72** system packs **72 Rubin GPUs** and **36 Vera CPUs** on the Arm architecture, fully liquid-cooled with modular, cable-less trays that install in about five minutes instead of two hours.

Nvidia says that versus GB200 NVL72, the platform trains models with **four times fewer GPUs** and cuts the cost per million tokens for agentic inference roughly **tenfold**.
