Open-source AI is taking market share faster than expected. The share of open-source models in token consumption on **Vercel** jumped from **28% to 62% in just two months**, according to investor Gavin Baker.

The reason is increasingly clear: closed models remain strong but expensive, while open ones are rapidly closing the quality gap. Open source adds another advantage — **control**: models can run locally, keeping data in-house with no dependence on third-party APIs, pricing, or access policies.

The price/quality/control ratio keeps improving. If the trend continues, the question shifts from "why run AI locally?" to "why hand over data and pay for a closed API when open source is already good enough?"
