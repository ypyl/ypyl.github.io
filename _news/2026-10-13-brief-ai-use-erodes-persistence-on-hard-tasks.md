Researchers from **UC Berkeley, MIT, and Oxford** found that ten minutes of working with an LLM reduced participants' ability to solve math and logic problems on their own. In the experiment with **1,200 participants**, one group used ChatGPT and then lost access to it; after the assistant was cut off, those participants skipped more questions and gave up more often than a control group that never used AI. The authors attribute the effect to the disappearance of **productive struggle**, the intellectual effort needed to build difficult skills, because getting used to ready answers makes independent problem solving feel inefficient and tiring. They propose building AI as a mentor that provokes reasoning instead of handing over results, and released the experiment code on GitHub.
