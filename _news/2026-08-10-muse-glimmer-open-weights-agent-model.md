**Muse Glimmer** is a 30-billion-parameter model with open weights (Apache 2.0) built for continuous agentic workloads on local hardware. It targets systems with **24 GB VRAM** and focuses on long-running agent workflows rather than one-off queries, with developers reporting strong results on agentic benchmarks in its class. GGUF quantizations are already available on Hugging Face, alongside an official guide for local deployment. Separately, **Muse Spark 1.2** is in the works, with its weights promised to be opened later.
