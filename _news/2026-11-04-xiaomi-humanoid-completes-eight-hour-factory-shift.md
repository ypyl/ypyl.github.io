At the **World Internet Conference 2026**, Xiaomi reported two robotics milestones. A humanoid worked its first **eight-hour shift** at a parts-sorting station, hitting the production quota, correcting its own errors, and coordinating with other equipment on the line. Separately, home-assistant robots running the **Xiaomi-Robotics-1** model were tested in the **Xiaomi Youth Apartments** complex, where one agent-driven unit completed more than **50 tasks in 30 minutes** while moving between three rooms. The **Xiaomi Robotics** division also collected a technology-leadership award at the event.
