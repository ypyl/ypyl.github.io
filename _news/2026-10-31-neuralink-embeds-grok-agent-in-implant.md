Neuralink has built an **agent into its brain implant**: the user thinks the phrase "Hey, Neuralink" and frames the task mentally, then **Grok** takes over the operating system and performs the steps on its own. The update was tested in a **72-hour session at the home** of a patient with **ALS** rather than in a lab visit.

The same release adds speech-intent recognition and a direct **iPhone** link, so a paralyzed person can call family or order food by thought, with brain signals routed into the phone's speech synthesizer. Neuralink also reported results from its core communication trial: in the first week, the patient typed **75 English words per minute** with a **5.8% word error rate**.
