Thinking Machines Lab, the company founded by former OpenAI CTO Mira Murati, has released **Inkling 2 Small**, a Mixture-of-Experts model with **594 billion total parameters, 17 billion active**, image inputs, up to **1M tokens** of context, and an adjustable reasoning effort. It scores **31 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index**, well above the first Inkling generation, with the largest gains in agentic coding, vision and knowledge work, and it uses vision in the loop to check and fix the interfaces it generates.

On the **November 5 DesignArena Website leaderboard** the model holds **1,340 points**, third overall behind only the closed **Muse Wonder (1,366)** and **Muse Spark 1.3 (1,351)**, and it is set to be the top open-weights entry once its weights ship. For now it runs on **Tinker** for inference and fine-tuning with closed weights; Thinking Machines plans to publish the full checkpoints under **Apache 2.0** next week after external safety testing and red-teaming. Despite the name, 17 billion active parameters do not shrink the 594 billion weights enough to run on an ordinary desktop.
