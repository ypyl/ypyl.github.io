OpenAI will retire the **Advanced** and **Standard** voice modes from ChatGPT Voice on 11 December 2026, its help centre states. Users of both modes are moved to GPT-Live automatically, and previous conversations stay in history where they can be continued by text or voice.

The modes differ in how they handle audio. Standard Voice transcribed the speech first and then voiced a text model's answer, while Advanced Voice processed audio directly but waited for the user to stop talking. GPT-Live listens and speaks at the same time. Advanced Voice survives only for sharing video and screen inside a call, so the shutdown is not complete.

OpenAI says voice usage nearly doubled after GPT-Live launched in July, and that almost every ChatGPT Voice user now picks it.
