In a rare convergence, the three most prominent figures in the American AI race have within days of each other called for decelerating frontier model development. Anthropic CEO **Dario Amodei** urged slowing down so that safety and governance can keep pace with model capabilities. OpenAI CEO **Sam Altman** supported independent safety evaluation and coordination mechanisms, while **Elon Musk** publicly agreed with the general direction. The core problem remains: no single company can slow down if competitors accelerate. Without global coordination, any unilateral U.S. pause becomes a strategic risk, particularly given China's parallel AI programs.
