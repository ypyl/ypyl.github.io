Anthropic has submitted a **Form S-1 registration statement** to the US Securities and Exchange Commission, moving its IPO from the confidential draft filed in June to a public filing. The company plans to list **Class A shares** on the **Nasdaq Global Select Market** under the ticker **ANT**, with **Goldman Sachs** and **Morgan Stanley** as lead underwriters.

Valuation and share price are not yet set. The filing is only the first formal step: no shares can be sold until the SEC declares the registration statement effective, and the listing depends on market conditions.
