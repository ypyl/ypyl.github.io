Z.ai has released the **open weights of GLM-5.3**, its strongest model, tuned for **agentic coding and defensive cybersecurity**. The weights can be downloaded, run locally, and fine-tuned for custom tasks — a full weight release rather than API-only access, so developers and researchers can inspect the model deeply and integrate it into their own pipelines.
