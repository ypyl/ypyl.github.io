OpenAI introduced **ChatGPT Wallet** on November 13, built with American Express, Mastercard and Visa, so agents in ChatGPT Work, Codex and dots can complete purchases while the card number, security code and expiry stay hidden from both the merchant and the agent. The user adds a card once, approves each purchase with a biometric check such as Face ID or a fingerprint scan, and the payment network returns a token that the merchant charges instead of the real card details. Card rewards and dispute protections for eligible claims survive the swap, because the customer still transacts directly with the business.

It is live in the **dots** service for ChatGPT Pro subscribers in the US with cards from US banks, and OpenAI shipped a **Commerce Partner Playbook** telling merchants how to avoid blocking agent traffic and how to make risk systems accept agent-originated orders. Delegated spending with pre-approved limits and categories is described as a future step, not a shipping feature.
