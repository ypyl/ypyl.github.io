China's AI stock rally has reversed, wiping **$480 billion** off the sector in under four months, according to Reuters. The **STAR50** index fell **40%**, surrendering two years of gains, robot maker **Unitree** lost half its value, and the blue-chip **CSI300** sits roughly **20%** below its June peak.

Investors are selling on stretched valuations and doubts that AI demand will justify them, with possible US export restrictions on Chinese technology adding pressure. Even after the drop, STAR50 trades at about **100 times** the earnings of its members, against roughly **30 times** for the Nasdaq 100.
