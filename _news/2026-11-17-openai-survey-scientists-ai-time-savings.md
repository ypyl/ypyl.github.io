title: "OpenAI Survey of 1,819 Scientists Puts AI Time Savings at 47%"
date: 2026-11-17

OpenAI analysed more than **580,000 messages** from **1,819 scientists** enrolled in its ChatGPT for Academic Researchers program and published the results as "Science at the Frontier". The model was used across the research workflow: 68% of participants used it to develop research ideas, and others for literature review, data analysis, code and drafting publications.

The headline saving is self-reported. Respondents estimated that the same work at comparable quality would have taken **1.9 times longer** without AI, a 47% reduction, and 98% expect AI to speed up discovery in their field.

OpenAI says staff analysed request categories without reading the conversations. A participant estimate is not a measured comparison, so the number describes perceived speed, not a benchmark of output.
