Anthropic priced its initial public offering at **$83 per share** on November 18, above the **$70 to $78** range it set a week earlier. The offering is **980 million shares**, of which 956.4 million are new and 23.6 million come from selling stockholders, for about **$81.3 billion** in gross proceeds. A 30-day option on a further 147 million shares would take the total to **$93.5 billion**.

Press reports put the implied valuation at about **$1.5 trillion**, against the $1.3 trillion to $1.45 trillion implied by the earlier 825 million share range. Trading starts on Nasdaq on November 19 under the ticker **ANT**, and the offering is expected to close on November 20. The SEC declared the registration statement effective the day before pricing.
