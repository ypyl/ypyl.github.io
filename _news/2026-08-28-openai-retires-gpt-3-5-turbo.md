OpenAI is shutting down **GPT-3.5 Turbo** — the model that introduced millions of people to the ChatGPT era. Per the retirement schedule, **gpt-3.5-turbo-1106** will be discontinued on **September 28, 2026**, and **gpt-3.5-turbo-0125** on **October 23, 2026**. Launched in late 2022, GPT-3.5 Turbo showed the mass audience that AI could chat, write texts, code, and solve tasks — and less than four years later, one of the most iconic models in generative AI history is bowing out for good.
