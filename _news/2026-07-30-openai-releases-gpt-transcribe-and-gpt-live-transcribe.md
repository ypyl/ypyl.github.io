OpenAI has introduced two new speech recognition models: **GPT Transcribe** for batch audio processing (up to 34x faster than real-time) and **GPT Live Transcribe** for low-latency streaming transcription. Both are available via API, support multilingual input, and take text context and keywords into account. According to Artificial Analysis benchmarks, GPT Transcribe achieves a **3.3% word error rate (WER)**—0.7 percentage points better than its predecessor GPT-4o Transcribe, though still behind the category leader Fun-Realtime-ASR-Preview from Alibaba Group at 1.7%. OpenAI has also cut pricing to **$0.0045 per minute** for GPT Transcribe and $0.017 per minute for the live version.
