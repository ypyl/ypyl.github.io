Google has launched **Gemini 3.8 Flash** and **Gemini 3.8 Flash Cyber**, two new models optimized for multimodal analysis of complex data. The base version scores 73.7% on the DeepSWE 1.1 coding benchmark, nearly matching Claude Opus 5's 74%, but at significantly lower API pricing: $0.75/M input and $3.75/M output tokens through the end of 2026. **Gemini 3.8 Flash Cyber** is a security-focused variant for automated vulnerability discovery that generates 2.6 times more correct Chrome patches than competing commercial models, with access restricted to trusted partners through the **Fairwind** program.
