OpenAI has updated its API pricing for the GPT-5.6 lineup and added rates for new products. Per 1 million tokens with standard (short) context, **GPT-5.6 Sol** now costs **$4 input / $20 output**, **Terra** **$2 / $12**, and **Luna** **$0.20 / $1.20** — making Luna roughly 17x cheaper than Sol on output. Long-context pricing is higher: Sol $8/$30, Terra $4/$18, Luna $0.40/$1.80, while cached input costs 10x less than standard input.

The page also lists **gpt-5.6-cyber** at **$12.50 / $75** per million tokens for security tasks, plus **web search at $10 per 1,000 calls** and **$0.03 per 20 minutes for a 1 GB container**.
