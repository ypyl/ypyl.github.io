Thailand's **Ministry of Digital Economy and Society** has launched **TH-AI Passport**, a state platform that provides free access to the **Pro versions** of services from **14 global AI providers**. The hub hosts **33 models** covering code, text, images, music, video, and data analysis. Registration is open only to Thai citizens aged **15 and older**. The launch runs parallel to the drafting of Thailand's first AI law, where the local IT industry is pushing for user data protection and support for domestic startups to reduce the country's dependence on foreign technology.
