The authors of **TheoPhysEval**, a benchmark of **50 unpublished research-level problems in theoretical physics**, report that on several tasks their initial reference solutions turned out to be incorrect. After the fixes, outside experts confirmed that **GPT-6 Astra** had answered correctly.

Running in **Codex** with tools and web search, Astra solved **49 of 50** problems for a score of **98%**, at an average cost of under **$3 per problem** and without human hints. A typical task would take a narrow specialist up to a full working day.

The authors are explicit about scope: the benchmark tests difficult calculations using known methods, and creating new physical theories demands more. Still, they argue, an assistant that makes experts correct their own reference answers is worth attention.
