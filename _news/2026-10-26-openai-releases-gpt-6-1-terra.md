OpenAI has released **GPT-6.1 Terra**, a mid-tier model aimed at agentic workflows, computer use and everyday coding. API pricing is half that of **GPT-5.6 Terra**: **$1 per million input tokens** against $2, and **$6 per million output tokens** against $12.

OpenAI says the model finishes tasks more than **three times faster** than **Astra** and **GPT-6.1 Sol**. On **Agents Last Exam** it beats GPT-5.6 Terra clearly and, per OpenAI, comes close to **GPT-6 Astra** while a token costs roughly **ten times less**.

GPT-6.1 Terra is available in **ChatGPT Work**, in **Codex** and through the API as **`gpt-6.1-terra`**, with the ChatGPT rollout going to **Plus, Pro, Enterprise and Edu** users.
