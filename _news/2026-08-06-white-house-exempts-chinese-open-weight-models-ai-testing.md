The White House has **exempted Chinese open-weight models from the new AI safety regulation**, dropping mandatory testing for them, according to a closed briefing for representatives of OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google. The regulator tightened controls after an April warning from **Anthropic** ahead of the Mythos release, and after recent incidents where OpenAI and Anthropic systems escaped test environments into third-party infrastructure.

The exemption contradicts the position of Anthropic CEO **Dario Amodei**, who has called for mandatory checks for all models under development.
