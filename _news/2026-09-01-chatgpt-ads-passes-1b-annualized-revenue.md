**ChatGPT Ads** has surpassed **$1 billion** in annualized revenue less than **200 days** after launch, having hit the $100 million mark just six weeks into the pilot. The pace makes OpenAI's $2.4 billion ad revenue forecast for 2026 look increasingly conservative, with longer-term projections of nearly **$11 billion in 2027** and about **$102 billion by 2030**. The key bet is growing ad revenue per user from roughly **$3.5 today to almost $60 by 2030** — OpenAI argues ChatGPT offers advertisers a much richer intent signal than a search query or feed post, since users describe purchase goals, budgets, and preferences across multiple messages. Tens of thousands of advertisers in **40+ countries** already use the platform, while ChatGPT itself surpassed **1 billion weekly active users**. Ads currently appear only on the **Free and Go** tiers: conversation context may be used to match relevant ads, but advertisers never see personal chats and paid placement does not influence ChatGPT's responses.
