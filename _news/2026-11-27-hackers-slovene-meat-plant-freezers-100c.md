title: "Hackers Set a Slovenian Meat Plant's Freezers to 100 °C"
date: 2026-11-27

---

Salamerija Alpija, the meat plant of the Tolmin agricultural cooperative in Slovenia, found several fridges and freezer chambers set to 100 °C shortly after noon, [24ur reported](http://24ur.com/novice/slovenija/hekerski-napad-na-hladilnike.html). The alarm never fired because the setpoint had been changed inside the control software rather than at the hardware, and a fridge in one of the cooperative's shops was found at 20 °C instead of its usual 2 to 3.

About EUR 250,000 of stock was at risk and none of it spoiled, because an employee caught the reading by chance before the air had time to warm. The plant's service technician then found his login to the cooling system blocked, with the screen showing a photo of Italian minister Matteo Salvini captioned "Thank Uncle Matteo".

The cooperative disconnected every cooling unit from the network and now checks temperatures by hand. Police in Nova Gorica opened a case for unauthorised access to a remote management system, and the plant is one of a wave: dozens of stores across Belgium, Luxembourg, Sweden, Ireland, the Netherlands and Estonia had freezers pushed to 50 °C in early November, after French supermarket chains were hit in October.
