Scientists have known for over 100 years that centrosome abnormalities are linked to cancer, but these tiny structures were nearly impossible to analyze at tumor scale. A new AI system analyzed **330,000 centrosomes** from breast cancer patients and found that what researchers had treated as a single type of anomaly is actually **two different processes** — one of which tracks with more aggressive tumors and worse patient survival. The discovery shows AI's ability to measure patterns in medical data that were previously effectively invisible.
