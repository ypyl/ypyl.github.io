SpaceX's AI division, the **Super Intelligence** group formed after the $250 billion all-stock purchase of xAI in February, cut roughly 60 roles from the team behind **Grok Imagine** over the past week, [The Information reported](https://www.theinformation.com/articles/elon-musks-ai-group-guts-grok-video-generation-team) on November 13. The video and image generator stays live, maintained by a small crew instead of a full engineering team.

The stated priorities are AI coding tools and the autonomous agent product **Grok Bot**, the same direction the group took with its $60 billion acquisition of Cursor in August. Video generation is the expensive item in that portfolio: a clip costs far more compute than a text request, and OpenAI had already discontinued Sora earlier this year on cost grounds. Musk had framed that exit in March as a competitive opening, promising an epic Grok Imagine release.
