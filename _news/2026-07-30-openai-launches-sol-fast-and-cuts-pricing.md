OpenAI has introduced **Sol Fast mode** for GPT-5.6 Sol, which runs up to **2.5x faster** than the standard mode at roughly double the cost—about **25% cheaper per unit of speed**. Simultaneously, the company reduced API pricing across its model lineup: **Luna** now costs $0.20/$1.20 per million input/output tokens, **Terra** drops to $2/$12, and **Sol** to $5/$30.

With these changes, **Luna is now 25x cheaper than Sol**, targeting high-volume tasks like classification, data processing, support bots, and simple agents. Sol Fast is aimed at latency-sensitive use cases: interactive products, complex agents, and real-time workflows. OpenAI is pushing on both fronts—cheap inference for scale and accelerated flagship models for performance.
