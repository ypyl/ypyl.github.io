Anthropic has released **Claude Fable 5.2** and **Claude Mythos 5.2**, two models sharing one base model and split only by their safeguards: Fable is generally available, Mythos goes to vetted organizations working in cybersecurity and biomedical research. Fable 5.2 is available through Claude, the API, AWS, Google Cloud, and Microsoft Azure.

- **Mathematics:** 1,000 agents formalized a proof of Fermat's Last Theorem in Lean in **30 hours**, against **11 days** for the previous research version of Claude.
- **Science and hardware:** Fable 5.2 solved two open problems in black hole physics and designed three working devices, including boards, enclosures, and software.
- **Cybersecurity:** Mythos 5.2 finds **10.2% more vulnerabilities** than Opus 5.5 on Anthropic's internal test.
- **Coding:** more than **90%** of the changes merged into Anthropic's main codebase are now written by Claude with human involvement.

Pricing is **$8 per million input tokens** and **$40 per million output tokens**, up to 30% faster than the previous generation. A **Fast Mode** runs up to 2.5x faster at $16/$80.
