Moonshot AI is aiming the Kimi models at scientific discovery, the South China Morning Post reported on November 16, putting the Beijing company in the same race as US labs trying to turn AI into a research engine. It is exploring biology, chemistry and pharmaceutical research rather than another round of coding tools.

The reasoning came from Giacomo Ficari, Moonshot's director of international, at Web Summit in Lisbon: finance and legal work ask for about the same level of intelligence as coding, and "science is actually bringing the level of intelligence even higher". The company gave no timeline, no benchmark and no product name for the work.

SCMP also reports, citing sources, that Moonshot has filed for a Hong Kong IPO, which would put the science pitch in front of public investors rather than only internal roadmaps.
