title: "Microsoft Adds MAI Cyber 1.1 Flash to Its MDASH Security Platform"
date: 2026-10-29
---

Microsoft has introduced **MAI Cyber 1.1 Flash**, a model built into its **MDASH** security platform to analyse source code. Inside the platform's multi-agent testing system it carries the bulk of bug search and verification, handing only non-trivial cases to larger models, which cut scanning cost by nearly a fifth with no loss in detection quality.

Microsoft reports that 90% of customers who once ran an audit through MAI Cyber stay on that profile instead of a mix of OpenAI models, and that about half of the checks in MDASH now run on the company's own model. Access is open to verified users of the platform.
