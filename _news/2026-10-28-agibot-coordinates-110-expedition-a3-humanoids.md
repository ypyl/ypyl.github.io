date: 2026-10-28

AgiBot has demonstrated **LinkBots**, a fleet-management platform that ran **110 production Expedition A3 full-size humanoids** in sync. Coordination to centimetre accuracy came from combining LinkBots with **ultra-wideband positioning** rather than from on-board vision alone. The company plans to deploy the A3 among people in services, tourism, science and education. It also cited industry figures of more than **40,000 humanoids produced in China in the first half of 2026**, against a near-term target of **100,000 units a year**.
