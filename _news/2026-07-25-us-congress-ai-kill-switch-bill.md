US lawmakers have introduced a bill requiring AI companies to implement emergency kill switches that the Department of Homeland Security could activate when systems pose a threat to human life or the national economy. The legislation defines loss of control as any situation where an AI performs actions not intended by its developers, citing recent incidents of autonomous agent behaviour and the cyberattack on Hugging Face infrastructure as motivating factors.
