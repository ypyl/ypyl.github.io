**Time** magazine has deployed a content-cloaking scheme that serves a special Markdown version of its pages to AI crawlers, stuffed with promotional material, while regular visitors and Google's bot see the standard HTML. Built with ad startup **Mobian**, the hidden text — including marketing for bank Ally — is read by LLMs as clean data, leading chatbots to organically recommend the brand in conversations about personal finance. The publishers openly admit the motive: planting the right wording into an AI's knowledge base lets them bias responses for an entire service's audience. The move marks the birth of a shadow AI-optimization market where brands pay to appear in generative search results.
