Tencent has released **Hy4-preview**, a new open-source model with a MoE architecture: **770B total parameters**, **49B active per token**, and context up to **1 million tokens**, with a focus on coding, agentic tasks, and productivity. It uses **Gated DeepSeek Sparse Attention** and **IndexCache** to lower the cost of long context and reuse sparse indexes across layers. Tencent positions Hy4 as a frontier-level open model with affordable deployment costs, licensed under **Apache 2.0**.
