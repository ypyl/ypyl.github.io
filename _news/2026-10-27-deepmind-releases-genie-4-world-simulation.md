date: 2026-10-27

---

Google DeepMind has introduced **Genie 4**, a world-simulation model that generates an interactive scene from a prompt and lets you move through it, steer the camera, and change events with text commands such as adding an object, altering the weather, or introducing a character.

- **Sound**: ambient noise, music, and dialogue are generated together with the world instead of added afterwards.
- **Physics**: characters react to events, and the model reproduces collisions and other interactions with more realistic motion.
- **Gemini control**: Gemini watches the scene, breaks a multi-step event into actions, checks whether each one happened, and adjusts the plan as the simulation runs.

DeepMind names game prototyping, film pre-visualization, and training environments for robots and AI agents as target uses. Genie 4 is an experimental model limited to trusted testers, with wider access planned for **Google AI Ultra** subscribers through Project Genie and for early-access partners in the Gemini API and AI Studio; a waitlist is open.
