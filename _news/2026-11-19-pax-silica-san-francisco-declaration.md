The United States and nine other Pax Silica participants signed the **San Francisco Declaration on the Secure Foundations of Enduring Prosperity** on November 15, at the coalition's founders retreat, convened by Under Secretary of State for Economic Affairs Jacob Helberg.

The declaration states that economic security is national security, fixes a shared definition of the sensitive technologies and critical infrastructure behind superintelligence supply chains, and resolves to create five working groups: pro-innovation policy frameworks, adoption of the technology in key sectors, supply chain cooperation across the stack, safe harbors and supply chain security hubs, and security and resilience.

The State Department release does not list the nine co-signers; the channel names Australia, the United Kingdom, Israel, India, Qatar, the UAE, South Korea, Singapore and Japan.
