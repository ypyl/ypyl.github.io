World Labs has removed the invitation gate from **Atlas**, its world model: anyone can now create and edit virtual worlds in the web tools at [atlas.worldlabs.ai](https://atlas.worldlabs.ai/), and developers get an API for scene generation, video, and 3D reconstruction. Until now the public site only let people view finished scenes, and building your own required a test application.

The two tools work in opposite directions:

- **Viewfinder** builds the space beyond the frame from one or more uploaded photos, after which the camera can be placed anywhere to shoot stills and video from new angles.
- **Chisel** starts from a rough blockout of walls and objects and generates images from that layout.

Both save the result as a **Gaussian Splatting** scene and convert it into polygonal meshes with real geometry that open in Blender and game engines. A world made from your own photos can be walked straight away in **Frameboy**, the real-time exploration tool announced earlier in November.

World Labs showed Atlas in September. The startup is preparing to become part of **AMD**, a deal it expects to close before the end of the year.
