**OpenAI** has notified **SpaceX** that it is terminating the contract that gave the **Cursor** coding environment access to its APIs, with access cut off on **November 12, 2026**. The creator of ChatGPT deliberately chose the farthest date permitted by the current agreement to give developers time to migrate. OpenAI cites distrust of Elon Musk's corporate structures — pointing to repeated terms-of-service violations by X and xAI — and says it is closing access to its technologies for security reasons ahead of the release of its **Astra** model.
