{%- comment -%} Fixed quote bar, modelled on Mantine's Marquee (Marquee.module.css): the track holds two copies of the list and translates by one copy width, so the loop has no seam and no scrollbar. It always scrolls, like Mantine's pauseOnHover=false default; set quotes.respect_reduced_motion to stop it for readers who ask. No prices are rendered here. Every number comes from assets/js/quotes.js, which fetches site.quotes.endpoint; the bar stays `hidden` until the first successful response, and hides again if the request fails. Nothing is cached in the page. List entries come from site.quotes.tickers in _config.yml, either a symbol or {symbol, name}.
